Privacy Policy — Mesori

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Developer: Omar Mahmoud Ahmed · Contact: omar.mahmoud.ahmed.01@gmail.com

Mesori is an educational game about ancient Egyptian history. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you control it.

1. Data we collect

DataPurpose
Email and password (password is hashed by the auth service; we never see it), or basic Google account data (email, account ID) if you sign in with GoogleAccount creation and sign-in
Username, avatar character, age, gender, countryProfile setup and personalization
Quiz progress, points, badges, answers, time spent in the app, share countSaving progress, leaderboard, badges
1v1 match data (opponents, answers, results, rating)Running matches and deciding winners
Player-to-player messages (up to 500 characters) and in-app notificationsPlayer communication. Automatically deleted after 7 days
Abuse reports you submit (reason, optional details, and a copy of the reported message and username)Protecting players and reviewing violations. Deleted after 180 days at most, and may remain even if the reporting or reported account is deleted

We do not collect: precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or payment data. We do not use our own analytics or tracking tools.

Ads: The app may show interstitial ads (between stages, subject to a time interval). It does not show banner ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob; when ads are shown, Google may automatically collect from your device: IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), the Android advertising ID, the app set ID, and identifiers related to accounts on the device where applicable, your interactions with ads, and performance and diagnostic information — for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not send your name, email, or in-app progress to Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization at any time in Android settings → Google → Ads. We limit ad content to a level suitable for teens, and in the EEA and the UK we ask for your consent before showing ads (through a Google consent form) which you can change later under “Settings → Ad & privacy choices”; we also request non-personalized ads for users under the digital age of consent in Europe. We can turn ads off entirely at any time.

On your device only: your login session and your last leaderboard rank snapshot (to show rank changes). Notifications, such as a reminder after 48 hours of inactivity, are scheduled and shown locally and do not send your data to any server.

2. What other players see

Other players see your username, avatar character, points, and rank. Your email, age, and country are not shown. Do not put your real name, phone number, or address in your username or messages. The app blocks messages that contain phone numbers, email addresses, or links, and you can report any player or message from inside the app.

Messaging between players is restricted: messages containing phone numbers, emails, links, or social handles are rejected, and you must accept the Terms of Use before your first message (we record the time and terms version). You can block any player (we store your block list to enforce it) and report any message or player; a copy of a reported message is kept for 180 days for review.

3. Storage and service providers

Service providers process data on our behalf only: Supabase (database and authentication), Cloudflare (web hosting), and Google (Google sign-in, AdMob ads, and Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when fonts load). We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers, and we disclose it only when required by law. The only exception is the data that Google AdMob collects automatically from your device when ads are shown (see section 1).

4. Minimum age

Mesori is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly allow accounts for anyone under 13. We ask for age before sign-up and again during profile setup. If we learn an account belongs to a user under 13, we will delete it and its data. If you are a parent and believe your child created an account, email us at the address above and we will delete it.

5. Retention

We keep your account data while your account exists and delete it when you delete the account. Messages and notifications are deleted after 7 days, and abuse reports after 180 days at most.

6. Your rights and account deletion

You may access, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for your data. To delete your account and all associated data immediately from inside the app: open Settings ⚙️, choose “Delete my account” (حذف حسابي), and confirm. If you cannot access the app: email omar.mahmoud.ahmed.01@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Mesori account" from the email registered to the account. We complete deletion within 30 days. Deleted: profile, progress, messages, badges, and matches against bots. Matches you played against other players remain in the opponent's history with no link to you (shown as a deleted account), and any match in progress ends as your forfeit. In-app deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

7. Security, changes, governing law

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is restricted with database-level security rules, but no system is 100% secure. We comply with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020. Material changes will be posted here with an updated date.
